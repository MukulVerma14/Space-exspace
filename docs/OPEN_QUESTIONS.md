# Open Questions & Client Clarifications

This document tracks ambiguities, unconfirmed brief statements, and open architectural choices for SPACE EXSPACE Company. These items must not be decided silently in code; they must be raised with the client or domain stakeholders.

| # | Topic | Question / Ambiguity | Context / Impact | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fleet Scale** | Is the equipment fleet ~145 active items or 5,000 items? | The brief mentions 5,000, but local data indicates ~145 items. For 145 items, lightweight indexing and standard SQL views perform in sub-milliseconds. For 5,000+ items with high-concurrency GPS/movement logs, we may need partitioned log tables and materialized views with refresh triggers. | **OPEN** |
| 2 | **Odoo Alignment** | Which Odoo features should it resemble? | Should workflows specifically emulate Odoo Rental (time-based rate cards and return inspections), Odoo Inventory (stock movement ledgers and scrap/adjustment types), or Odoo Invoicing/Accounting? Knowing which workflows need parity guides UI terminology and API boundaries. | **OPEN** |
| 3 | **Chatbot Scope ("Books")** | What does "books" refer to in the chatbot feature request? | Does "books" mean standard operating equipment manuals/safety PDFs, accounting general ledgers, or historical rental records? | **OPEN** |
| 4 | **Invoice Workflow & Generation** | What is the exact invoice scope: store uploaded invoices only, or generate customer invoices? | The prototype assumes both supplier invoices (received) and customer invoices (issued) exist, and that invoices are stored/recorded rather than generated from scratch. If customer invoice generation or ZATCA e-invoicing (Phase 2 XML/QR generation) is required later, a dedicated PDF generation and cryptographic signing pipeline will be needed. | **OPEN** |
| 5 | **Application Delivery: PWA vs Native App** | Is a mobile-first responsive PWA sufficient, or are native iOS/Android apps required? | Currently assumed as a mobile-first PWA accessible via mobile browser with HTML5 camera QR scanning. If offline caching in remote desert yards without cellular signal is required, native apps or ServiceWorker background sync will be needed. | **OPEN** |
| 6 | **User Access Scope: Staff-Only vs Customer Portal** | Will external customers ever log in to track their rented equipment? | The prototype strictly assumes internal staff access only (two roles: Admin and Staff). If client portal access is required to view rental status, proof-of-delivery, or invoices, a third role (`customer`) with strict row-level security must be introduced. | **OPEN** |
| 7 | **Website & Domain Integration** | What is the production website domain and hosting topology? | It is assumed that this equipment tracker is a standalone application with its own domain/address, and that the company website only links to it. Does the client plan to host it on a subdomain (e.g. `tracker.spaceexspace.sa`) or embed it into an existing company portal? | **OPEN** |
| 8 | **Localization & Arabic** | Is Arabic (and RTL UI) required for yard staff? | Most field technicians in Saudi Arabia may prefer or legally require Arabic interfaces. The prototype is English-only; internationalization (i18n) keys will be needed if Arabic is confirmed. | **OPEN** |
| 9 | **VAT Inclusivity** | Are standard rate cards quoted VAT-inclusive or VAT-exclusive? | Saudi VAT is 15%. Invoices store explicit `vat_amount` and `subtotal`, but whether rental quotation rates shown to clients already include VAT affects billing calculations. | **OPEN** |
| 10 | **ZATCA Phase 2** | What are the timeline and integration expectations for ZATCA e-invoicing? | Out of scope for this prototype, but production rollout in Saudi Arabia requires cryptographic stamp generation, XML UBL 2.1 formatting, and QR encoding (TLV format). | **OPEN** |
| 11 | **DNS Management & Domain Delegation** | Who manages DNS for `spaceexspace.com` (or `.sa`), and how will domain records be pointed to the server? | Caddy automates TLS certificate provisioning via Let's Encrypt / ZeroSSL, but requires public DNS A/AAAA records resolving to the host's public IP address before certificates can be issued. | **OPEN** |
| 12 | **Offsite S3 Storage & Cloud Ownership** | Does SPACE EXSPACE possess an AWS / S3-compatible cloud account for off-site database replication? | The daily backup script (`scripts/backup_daily.sh`) replicates encrypted database snapshots off-site via `rclone`. We need to determine if client IT will supply S3 bucket credentials (e.g. AWS Bahrain, Cloudflare R2, STC Cloud) or if the development team should provision one. | **OPEN** |
| 13 | **Scanner Hardware vs Mobile Phone Camera** | Will yard technicians scan QR labels using smartphone cameras or rugged hardware laser scanners? | In-browser camera scanning (`getUserMedia`) requires HTTPS Secure Context and camera permission prompts. Dedicated warehouse handhelds (e.g. Zebra, Honeywell) operate as keyboard wedges that type scanned asset codes directly into focused input fields, requiring no camera permissions. | **OPEN** |
| 14 | **Saudi Data Residency & Regulatory Compliance (PDPL)** | Does the company require physical database hosting inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia? | Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and NDMO guidelines, commercial inventory and client data may need to reside in-kingdom (e.g. local Saudi data centers such as Oracle Cloud Riyadh/Jeddah, STC Cloud, or Sahaba VPS) rather than European or US cloud regions. | **OPEN** |
